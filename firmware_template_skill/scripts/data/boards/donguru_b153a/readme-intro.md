The firmware was generated by the [embedded_bedrock](https://github.com/romixlab/embedded_bedrock) firmware
template and talks to the PC over the board's own USB, behind the hub on the receptacle J401, which also powers it;
a J-Link on the receptacle's SBU pins flashes it and carries its defmt log. `bedrock_fw.json` records the generator
answers; see AGENTS.md.
