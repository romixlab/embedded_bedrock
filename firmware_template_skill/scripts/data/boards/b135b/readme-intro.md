The firmware was generated by the [embedded_bedrock](https://github.com/romixlab/embedded_bedrock) firmware
template and talks to the PC over the board's own USB-C (J301) at high speed, through the USB3315 ULPI PHY; the
same cable powers it. A J-Link on the Tag-Connect J202 flashes it and carries its defmt log. `bedrock_fw.json`
records the generator answers; see AGENTS.md.
