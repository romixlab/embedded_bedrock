The firmware was generated by the [embedded_bedrock](https://github.com/romixlab/embedded_bedrock) firmware
template for the [Raspberry Pi Pico 2](https://www.raspberrypi.com/products/raspberry-pi-pico-2/) and talks to the
PC over its own micro-USB (`bedrock_fw.json` records the generator answers; see AGENTS.md). A
[Raspberry Pi Debug Probe](https://www.raspberrypi.com/products/debug-probe/) on J2 gives SWD and a second serial
port; BOOTSEL (hold the button, plug the USB in) gives drag-and-drop UF2 flashing without one.
